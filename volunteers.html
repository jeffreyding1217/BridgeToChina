For volunteers

Teach a conversation, one student at a time.

You do not need teaching experience. You need to be fluent in English, reliable, and kind.

Who can volunteer

  • People aged 13 or older who are fluent in English.
  • If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must give consent, and you will only be matched with students who are under 18.
  • No teaching experience is required.
  • You need a computer or phone, a camera, a microphone and a stable internet connection.

What you will do

  • Apply with a profile, short English and Chinese assessments and spoken prompts.
  • Complete a short orientation.
  • Teach 30-minute lessons using the lesson library: 120 topics with vocabulary, grammar, speaking practice and games.
  • Report each lesson after it happens.

Volunteer hours and certificates

After each lesson, you submit a short report. A program administrator checks it and approves the hours. Only approved hours appear on your certificate, which you can print or save as a PDF.

Hours are not automatic. They count after an administrator approves your report.

What we ask

  • Be on time and tell your student if you cannot make a lesson.
  • Keep conversations on topic and appropriate for the student’s age.
  • Never ask for or share personal contact details with a student, especially one under 18.
  • Use only the site and VooV Meeting to communicate.
