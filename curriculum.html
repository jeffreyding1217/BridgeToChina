Curriculum

60 lessons, three levels.

Every topic is a 30-minute lesson with vocabulary, a language focus with common mistakes, practice sentences, a speaking roleplay and a game.

Beginner

Basic vocabulary, sentence construction, everyday conversations and confidence. Chinese is used to explain basic words.

1
Introducing Yourself and Making FriendsSay who you are, where you are from and what you like.
2
Greetings and Common ExpressionsHello, goodbye, thanks, sorry, please.
3
Numbers, Dates, and TimeCount, say dates and tell the time.
4
Family Members and RelationshipsTalk about your family and who is who.
5
Colors, Shapes, and SizesDescribe things with color, shape and size.
6
Food, Drinks, and Favorite MealsTalk about food and order what you like.
7
Animals and PetsDescribe animals and talk about pets.
8
School Subjects and Classroom VocabularyTalk about classes and use classroom English.
9
Daily Routines and ActivitiesDescribe a normal day with the present simple.
10
Hobbies and Free-Time ActivitiesTalk about what you like to do in your free time.
11
Describing People and AppearanceDescribe how people look.
12
Describing Places and LocationsDescribe where things are using there is / there are.
13
Weather and SeasonsTalk about the weather and your favorite season.
14
Clothing and ShoppingTalk about clothes and buy things in a shop.
15
Body Parts and Basic Health VocabularyName body parts and say what is wrong.
16
Emotions and FeelingsSay how you feel and why.
17
Common Verbs and ActionsUse everyday verbs correctly.
18
Prepositions of Place and DirectionSay where things are and how to get there.
19
Asking and Answering Basic QuestionsMake questions with who, what, where, when, why, how.
20
Present, Past, and Future Tense BasicsUse verbs for today, yesterday and tomorrow.

Intermediate

More complex sentences, practical communication, storytelling and opinions. Mostly English.

21
Ordering Food at RestaurantsOrder politely, ask questions about the menu and pay the bill.
22
Traveling and TransportationPlan journeys and use transport vocabulary.
23
Asking for and Giving DirectionsFind your way politely.
24
Describing Past ExperiencesTell what happened using past tenses.
25
Talking About Future Plans and GoalsSay what you plan and hope to do.
26
Expressing Opinions and PreferencesSay what you think and give reasons.
27
Comparing People, Places, and ThingsUse comparatives and superlatives.
28
Movies, Television, and EntertainmentTalk about films and shows and recommend them.
29
Sports and Physical ActivitiesTalk about sports, teams and exercise.
30
Technology and Social MediaTalk about phones, apps and the internet.
31
Celebrations, Holidays, and TraditionsDescribe festivals and how you celebrate.
32
Shopping, Money, and BudgetingTalk about prices, saving and spending.
33
Making Plans and InvitationsInvite, accept and decline politely.
34
Giving Instructions and Explaining ProcessesExplain how to do things step by step.
35
Problem-Solving and Asking for HelpExplain a problem and ask for or give advice.
36
Describing Personal Experiences and MemoriesTalk about your life and memories.
37
Environmental Issues and NatureTalk about pollution, recycling and protecting nature.
38
Healthy Habits and LifestyleTalk about diet, exercise, sleep and stress.
39
Storytelling and Creative WritingTell a story with a clear beginning, middle and end.
40
Conditional Sentences (If Statements)Say what happens if, and what would happen if.

Advanced

Fluency and critical thinking: richer vocabulary and complex grammar. English only.

41
Debating and Defending OpinionsArgue a position, concede a point and rebut.
42
Current Events and World NewsDiscuss news and report what others say.
43
Culture and Cultural DifferencesCompare cultures respectfully.
44
Artificial Intelligence and TechnologyDiscuss opportunities and risks of AI.
45
Environmental Protection and Climate ChangeExplain causes, effects and solutions.
46
Education Systems Around the WorldCompare education systems and discuss improvements.
47
Careers, Jobs, and Professional CommunicationTalk about work and write professional messages.
48
Leadership and TeamworkDiscuss what makes good leaders and teams.
49
Psychology and Human BehaviorDiscuss why people think and act as they do.
50
Social Issues and Community ProblemsDiscuss problems in society and possible solutions.
51
Media Literacy and MisinformationEvaluate sources and spot fake news.
52
Financial Literacy and EconomicsTalk about money, saving, investing and the economy.
53
Scientific Discoveries and InnovationDescribe discoveries and inventions with the passive.
54
Literature and Analyzing StoriesAnalyze themes, characters and the author’s message.
55
Public Speaking and PresentationsGive a clear, confident presentation.
56
Persuasive Writing and ArgumentsWrite a clear, persuasive text.
57
Idioms, Slang, and Figurative LanguageUnderstand and use common idioms and expressions.
58
Complex Grammar and Sentence StructuresUse relative clauses, the passive, reported speech and participle clauses.
59
Hypothetical Situations and Critical ThinkingDiscuss ‘what if’ situations, past and present.
60
Independent Discussions and Group DebatesLead and join a discussion with confidence.
Volunteers use these lessons through the lesson library after they are approved. The curriculum is new, and we will keep improving it.
