Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Bridge to China is a free program that connects volunteer English speakers with students in mainland China for one-on-one conversation practice. This page explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain and short.

What we collect

Account: your email address and password (we never see your password in readable form).

Volunteer application: name, preferred name, date of birth, country, time zone, a short bio, languages, school or work, experience, availability, and your answers to the language assessments and orientation quiz.

Student application: name, date of birth, school grade, city or region, time zone, English level, learning goals, availability, your placement test answers, and, if you are under 18, a parent or guardian's contact details (such as email and phone).

While you take part: your match, lesson reports and hours, homework you type, messages you send to your partner, and the meeting details saved for your lessons.

Consent records: that you accepted our rules and, for people under 18, that a parent or guardian agreed, and when.

Technical: basic data your browser sends and login sessions, which our hosting and sign-in providers handle so the site works.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising.
  • We do not ask for payment details. The program is free.
  • We do not record lessons. We ask volunteers and students not to record them either.

How we use it

  • To review applications and match volunteers with students.
  • To run lessons: show your partner the information they need, keep lesson reports, and confirm volunteer service hours and certificates.
  • To keep people safe, including reviewing reports and removing anyone who breaks our rules.
  • To send emails about your account or application.

Who can see it

  • Our team (the administrators who review applications, matches and hours).
  • Your matched partner sees only what the program shows them: your name, country, short bio, level or goals, and the times you are available. Students and volunteers do not see each other's email address. Messages between a matched pair are visible to the two of them and to our team, and messages with students under 18 block phone numbers, emails and social media handles.
  • Service providers that help us run the site: Supabase (database and sign-in), GitHub Pages (website hosting), and an email provider for account emails. Lessons themselves take place on VooV Meeting or Tencent Meeting, which are run by Tencent under its own privacy policy.

Our providers may store data on servers outside your country, including outside mainland China. By using the site you understand your information may be handled in other countries. We may also share information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Children

Students under 18 need a parent or guardian to help them take part, and we ask for a guardian's permission. Volunteers must be at least 13; volunteers under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission and are only matched with students under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as we need it for lesson records, volunteer hours and safety. You can ask us to delete your account and information at any time. We may keep a small record where it is needed for safety or by law.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or ask a question about it, by contacting us. If you are a parent or guardian, you can ask on behalf of your child.

Security

We use sign-in security and database access rules so people only see what they should. No website can promise perfect security, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will tell account holders.

Contact

Please use our Contact page.
