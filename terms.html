Terms of Use

How the program works and what we expect from everyone.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

These terms explain how Bridge to China works and what we expect from everyone who takes part. By creating an account or using the site, you agree to them.

What the program is

Bridge to China is a free, volunteer-run program for English conversation practice. Lessons are 30-minute video calls between a volunteer and a student. It is not a school, it is not accredited, and we cannot promise a match, a schedule, or any improvement in English.

Who can take part

  • Volunteers must be at least 13. Volunteers under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission and are only matched with students under 18.
  • Students under 18 need a parent or guardian to help them and give permission.
  • Our team reviews applications by hand. We may decline, pause or end anyone's participation to keep the program safe.

Your account

Give accurate information, keep your password private, and do not share your account. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

Rules for everyone

  • Be kind and respectful. No harassment, bullying, hate or sexual content.
  • Keep all contact on the program's channels. Do not exchange phone numbers, social media accounts, or other private contact details, and do not arrange private meetings. Do not send gifts or ask for money or favors.
  • Do not record, screenshot or share lessons or other people's details without the program's permission.
  • Stay away from pressing opinions on sensitive topics such as politics or religion.
  • Tell our team promptly if something worries you. Do not try to handle a safety concern alone.

Lessons and schedule

Each student has one lesson per week with the same volunteer. Volunteers share when they are free, and a volunteer and student agree each lesson time. If you cannot make a lesson, tell your partner as early as you can. Lessons take place on a third-party video service (VooV Meeting or Tencent Meeting), and its own terms apply.

Volunteer hours and certificates

Hours count only after an administrator confirms the lesson report. A certificate shows the hours confirmed by Bridge to China. It is not an accredited qualification. We may remove hours that were reported incorrectly.

What you post

Your homework answers and messages belong to you. You allow us to store them and show them to your partner and our team so the program can run. Messages and homework may be reviewed by our team to keep people safe and to look into reports.

Lesson materials

The lesson library is provided for use in Bridge to China lessons. Please do not copy it or sell it.

No guarantees

We do our best, but the site and lessons are provided "as is". We are not responsible for what volunteers or students say or do, for problems with third-party services, or for losses that come from using a free, volunteer-run program, to the extent the law allows.

Changes

We may update these terms. If we do, we will change the date above, and keep using the site means you accept the new terms.

Contact

Please use our Contact page.
