How lessons work · 上课方式

Lessons happen on VooV Meeting. Once you are matched and a lesson is booked, your volunteer will send you a meeting link a few minutes before it starts. VooV Meeting does not work in a web browser. You must download the app first.
课程通过腾讯会议国际版 VooV Meeting 进行。匹配成功并预约课程后，志愿者会在开课前几分钟把会议链接发给你。VooV 无法在网页浏览器中使用，请务必先下载并安装应用。

  1. Download and install the app · 下载并安装 VooV Meeting 应用
  2. Wait for your volunteer's link · 等待志愿者发送链接
  3. Open the app at lesson time and join with the link or Meeting ID · 到时间打开应用，用链接或会议号加入
