For students

Practice speaking English with a real person.

Lessons are free. You talk with a volunteer for 30 minutes, one-on-one, over video.

What to expect

  • Free lessons, one student with one volunteer.
  • Each lesson is 30 minutes: warm-up, new words, grammar, speaking practice and a game.
  • Lessons are on VooV Meeting. VooV does not work in a web browser, so download and install the app first. You will need a camera, a microphone and a stable internet connection.
  • You can message your volunteer on the site after you are matched.

How to join

  • Create an account and fill in your application.
  • Take a short English placement so we know your level.
  • Our team reviews your application and matches you with a volunteer.

Students under 18 need a parent or guardian to help with the application.
