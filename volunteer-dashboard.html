When You Can Teach

Each student has one lesson per week with you. Click or drag on a day to mark when you are usually free (zoom in for 15-minute precision). Only times that are between 8 AM and 11 PM in Beijing can be picked, because that is when students are awake; the gray hours are blocked, and the left edge shows the matching Beijing time. Then add any dates you can't teach. Your students see these times in Beijing time, and the site warns you if you schedule outside them.
