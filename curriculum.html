Curriculum

120 lessons, three levels.

Every topic is a 30-minute lesson with vocabulary, a language focus with common mistakes, practice sentences, a speaking roleplay and a game.

Beginner (1–50)

Basic vocabulary, sentence construction, everyday conversations and confidence. Chinese is used to explain basic words.

1
Introducing Yourself and Making FriendsSay who you are, where you are from and what you like.
2
Greetings and Common ExpressionsHello, goodbye, thanks, sorry, please.
3
Numbers, Dates, and TimeCount, say dates and tell the time.
4
Family Members and RelationshipsTalk about your family and who is who.
5
Colors, Shapes, and SizesDescribe things with color, shape and size.
6
Food, Drinks, and Favorite MealsTalk about food and order what you like.
7
Animals and PetsDescribe animals and talk about pets.
8
School Subjects and Classroom VocabularyTalk about classes and use classroom English.
9
Daily Routines and ActivitiesDescribe a normal day with the present simple.
10
Hobbies and Free-Time ActivitiesTalk about what you like to do in your free time.
11
Describing People and AppearanceDescribe how people look.
12
Describing Places and LocationsDescribe where things are using there is / there are.
13
Weather and SeasonsTalk about the weather and your favorite season.
14
Clothing and ShoppingTalk about clothes and buy things in a shop.
15
Body Parts and Basic Health VocabularyName body parts and say what is wrong.
16
Emotions and FeelingsSay how you feel and why.
17
Common Verbs and ActionsUse everyday verbs correctly.
18
Prepositions of Place and DirectionSay where things are and how to get there.
19
Asking and Answering Basic QuestionsMake questions with who, what, where, when, why, how.
20
Present, Past, and Future Tense BasicsUse verbs for today, yesterday and tomorrow.
21
Fruits and VegetablesName common fruits and vegetables and say which ones you like.
22
Parts of a House and FurnitureName parts of a house and furniture and say where things are.
23
Rooms and Household ObjectsName rooms and objects in a home and say what we do in each room.
24
Transportation and VehiclesName vehicles and say how you travel.
25
Jobs and OccupationsName jobs and say what people do.
26
Places Around TownName places in a town and say what people do there.
27
Days of the Week and MonthsSay the days and months and talk about your week and birthday.
28
Telling the Time and Daily SchedulesTell the time and describe a daily schedule.
29
Common Household ChoresTalk about chores and who does them at home.
30
Toys and GamesName toys and games and say what you like to play.
31
School Supplies and StationeryName school supplies and ask to borrow things.
32
Breakfast, Lunch, and DinnerName meals and talk about what you eat each day.
33
Common Opposites (Big/Small, Hot/Cold)Learn opposite words and use them to describe things.
34
Asking for PermissionAsk for permission politely and respond with yes or no.
35
Making Simple RequestsMake polite requests and respond to them.
36
Describing Your Favorite ThingsDescribe your favorite things and say why you like them.
37
Likes, Dislikes, and FavoritesSay what you like, love, don’t like and hate.
38
Possessive Words (My, Your, His, Hers)Use my, your, his, her, our and their to show who owns things.
39
Singular and Plural NounsMake plurals correctly and use is / are with them.
40
Countable and Uncountable NounsTell countable and uncountable nouns apart and use some, any, much and many.
41
Using A, An, and TheUse a, an and the correctly.
42
Subject and Object PronounsUse I, you, he, she, it, we, they and me, you, him, her, it, us, them.
43
Question Words (Who, What, Where, When, Why, How)Ask questions with who, what, where, when, why and how.
44
There Is and There AreDescribe what exists in a place with there is, there are and their questions.
45
Can and CannotTalk about ability and permission with can and can’t.
46
Basic Adverbs of FrequencySay how often you do things with always, usually, often, sometimes and never.
47
Common Phrasal VerbsLearn common phrasal verbs for daily life.
48
Describing Objects Using AdjectivesDescribe things using size, shape, color and material.
49
Following Simple InstructionsUnderstand and give simple instructions and commands.
50
Talking About Your WeekendDescribe what you do on weekends and plan for the next one.

Intermediate (51–100)

More complex sentences, practical communication, storytelling and opinions. Mostly English.

51
Ordering Food at RestaurantsOrder politely, ask questions about the menu and pay the bill.
52
Traveling and TransportationPlan journeys and use transport vocabulary.
53
Asking for and Giving DirectionsFind your way politely.
54
Describing Past ExperiencesTell what happened using past tenses.
55
Talking About Future Plans and GoalsSay what you plan and hope to do.
56
Expressing Opinions and PreferencesSay what you think and give reasons.
57
Comparing People, Places, and ThingsUse comparatives and superlatives.
58
Movies, Television, and EntertainmentTalk about films and shows and recommend them.
59
Sports and Physical ActivitiesTalk about sports, teams and exercise.
60
Technology and Social MediaTalk about phones, apps and the internet.
61
Celebrations, Holidays, and TraditionsDescribe festivals and how you celebrate.
62
Shopping, Money, and BudgetingTalk about prices, saving and spending.
63
Making Plans and InvitationsInvite, accept and decline politely.
64
Giving Instructions and Explaining ProcessesExplain how to do things step by step.
65
Problem-Solving and Asking for HelpExplain a problem and ask for or give advice.
66
Describing Personal Experiences and MemoriesTalk about your life and memories.
67
Environmental Issues and NatureTalk about pollution, recycling and protecting nature.
68
Healthy Habits and LifestyleTalk about diet, exercise, sleep and stress.
69
Storytelling and Creative WritingTell a story with a clear beginning, middle and end.
70
Conditional Sentences (If Statements)Say what happens if, and what would happen if.
71
Making Phone Calls and Leaving MessagesStart and end phone calls politely and leave a clear message.
72
Making and Responding to ComplaintsComplain politely and respond to complaints calmly.
73
Visiting a Doctor and Describing SymptomsDescribe symptoms and understand the doctor’s advice.
74
Airport Conversations and Flight SituationsHandle check-in, security and boarding conversations.
75
Hotel Reservations and AccommodationBook a room and ask about hotel services.
76
Planning a VacationPlan a trip with details and give reasons.
77
Ordering Online and Online ShoppingDescribe orders, delivery and returns when shopping online.
78
Understanding Advertisements and CommercialsUnderstand how ads persuade and describe advertisements.
79
Describing Accidents and Unexpected EventsTell the story of an accident or surprise using past tenses.
80
Explaining Rules and ResponsibilitiesTalk about rules, obligations and permission with must, have to and can.
81
Giving Advice and SuggestionsGive advice and suggestions using should, could and had better.
82
Agreeing and Disagreeing PolitelyShare opinions and agree or disagree with respect.
83
Expressing Uncertainty and PossibilityTalk about things that are not certain using might, may, could and probably.
84
Describing Personality TraitsDescribe people’s characters and give examples.
85
Friendship and RelationshipsTalk about friends and relationships and how to keep them.
86
School Life and Academic ChallengesDescribe school life and talk about academic challenges.
87
Time Management and ProductivityTalk about how to manage time and get more done.
88
Making Decisions and Weighing OptionsCompare options and explain how you make decisions.
89
Causes and EffectsExplain why things happen and what results from them.
90
Explaining Reasons and ConsequencesGive detailed reasons and discuss possible consequences.
91
Describing Graphs, Charts, and DataDescribe trends and compare data in graphs and charts.
92
News Reports and Summarizing InformationSummarize news stories using who, what, where, when and why.
93
Understanding Different AccentsUnderstand speakers with different accents and ask for clarification.
94
Listening to and Summarizing ConversationsListen for the main ideas and summarize what you heard.
95
Writing Emails and Formal MessagesWrite clear, polite formal emails.
96
Writing Informal Letters and Personal MessagesWrite friendly letters and messages to people you know.
97
Relative Clauses (Who, Which, That)Add information with who, which and that.
98
Present Perfect TenseTalk about experiences and recent events with the present perfect.
99
Passive VoiceUse the passive to focus on the action or object.
100
Reported SpeechReport what someone said without using their exact words.

Advanced (101–120)

Fluency and critical thinking: richer vocabulary and complex grammar. English only.

101
Debating and Defending OpinionsArgue a position, concede a point and rebut.
102
Current Events and World NewsDiscuss news and report what others say.
103
Culture and Cultural DifferencesCompare cultures respectfully.
104
Artificial Intelligence and TechnologyDiscuss opportunities and risks of AI.
105
Environmental Protection and Climate ChangeExplain causes, effects and solutions.
106
Education Systems Around the WorldCompare education systems and discuss improvements.
107
Careers, Jobs, and Professional CommunicationTalk about work and write professional messages.
108
Leadership and TeamworkDiscuss what makes good leaders and teams.
109
Psychology and Human BehaviorDiscuss why people think and act as they do.
110
Social Issues and Community ProblemsDiscuss problems in society and possible solutions.
111
Media Literacy and MisinformationEvaluate sources and spot fake news.
112
Financial Literacy and EconomicsTalk about money, saving, investing and the economy.
113
Scientific Discoveries and InnovationDescribe discoveries and inventions with the passive.
114
Literature and Analyzing StoriesAnalyze themes, characters and the author’s message.
115
Public Speaking and PresentationsGive a clear, confident presentation.
116
Persuasive Writing and ArgumentsWrite a clear, persuasive text.
117
Idioms, Slang, and Figurative LanguageUnderstand and use common idioms and expressions.
118
Complex Grammar and Sentence StructuresUse relative clauses, the passive, reported speech and participle clauses.
119
Hypothetical Situations and Critical ThinkingDiscuss ‘what if’ situations, past and present.
120
Independent Discussions and Group DebatesLead and join a discussion with confidence.
Volunteers use these lessons through the lesson library after they are approved. The curriculum is new, and we will keep improving it.
