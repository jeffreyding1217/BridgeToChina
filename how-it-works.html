How it works

From sign-up to your first lesson.

Everything happens in a few steps. Nothing is automatic: people on our team review applications and make the matches.

For students

1

Create an account

Sign up with your email and confirm it.

2

Apply

Fill in your application and complete a short English placement. Students under 18 add a parent or guardian.

3

Get reviewed

Our team reviews every application. We may ask for more information.

4

Get matched

Once approved, we match you with a volunteer based on level, age group and free times.

5

Take lessons

Download the VooV app first (it does not work in a web browser). Your volunteer shares a VooV Meeting link. You can also message each other on the site.

For volunteers

1

Create an account

Sign up with your email and confirm it.

2

Apply and take the assessments

Complete your profile, plus short English and Chinese assessments, including some spoken prompts.

3

Complete orientation

A short orientation on teaching, cultural respect and student safety.

4

Get approved and matched

Our team reviews every application. Approved volunteers are matched with a student by our team.

5

Teach and report

Use the lesson library for each lesson. Report each lesson afterwards. An administrator approves your hours.

What a lesson looks like

Each lesson is 30 minutes. Volunteers follow a ready-made plan that they can share on screen during the call.

30-minute lesson

0–5 min
Conversation warm-upSimple questions about their day, hobbies and family.
5–10 min
VocabularyLearn new words and use them in sentences.
10–15 min
Grammar & sentence structureOne idea, with examples and practice.
15–23 min
Speaking practiceA real-life roleplay, such as ordering food or asking for directions.
23–27 min
Interactive gameA short game that uses today’s language.
27–30 min
Review & wrap-upWhat did we learn? A small homework task.

Where lessons happen

Lessons are video calls on VooV Meeting (the international version of Tencent Meeting), which works in mainland China. VooV does not work in a web browser, so everyone must download and install the VooV app first. The volunteer adds the meeting link on their dashboard, and the student sees a Join button.

We are matching people by hand, so there can be a wait between approval and your first match. We will not promise a date.
