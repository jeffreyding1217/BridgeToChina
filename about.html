About us

Language builds friendships.

Bridge to China connects students in China who want to practice conversational English with volunteers who speak English fluently.

Our mission

Our mission is to make good English conversation practice available to students who do not have many chances to speak with fluent English speakers, and to give volunteers a meaningful way to help from anywhere in the world.

We believe language builds friendships, cultural understanding and opportunity. Each lesson is a conversation between two people, not a lecture.

What we do

  • We match one volunteer with one student for regular 30-minute video lessons.
  • We give volunteers ready-made lesson plans for 120 topics, from beginner to advanced.
  • We review every volunteer and every student by hand before anyone is matched.
  • We keep records of lessons and approve volunteer service hours.

Our values

📘 Education
🤝 Inclusion
🌿 Respect
🌏 Cultural Exchange
💚 Community Service

How we keep students safe

  • Every volunteer application is reviewed by our team before matching. Volunteers complete language assessments and an orientation that covers student safety.
  • Volunteers aged 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian’s consent, and are only matched with students under 18.
  • Students under 18 provide parent or guardian details.
  • Messages between a volunteer and a student happen on this site and can be read by program administrators. For students under 18, emails, phone numbers and links are blocked in messages.
  • Matches are made by people on our team, not automatically.
We are a small, new program. If you have a safety concern, please contact us.
